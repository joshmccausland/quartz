### I. Origins and the Incident at Amrit-Kshara

Born in the twilight years of the Ephemero Age, Vaelen was a prodigy of the [[Ursine]], possessing an attunement to the "song" of the wood by his tenth winter. However, his curiosity extended beyond the groves of [[Sadhana]]. He was fascinated by the "Latecomers"—the humans, elves, and dwarves—who had begun gathering at the confluence of the **Marrow Path** and the **Vein River**.

Against the strict isolationist decrees of his [[Root-Kin]], Vaelen frequently observed the construction of the **Prism of the Vanguard**, a glass spire rising from the center of the lake then known as _Amrit-Kshara_.

One tragic night, Vaelen was discovered by human laborers under firelight. In the ensuing panic, the young ursine, blinded by fear and the dark, accidentally crushed a human worker against the stone. Retribution was halted only by the intervention of [[Auranythe]].

> "Fear killed this man. For had you shown acceptance to a child, his weight would have been a hug, not a hammer."

This mercy forged a lifelong loyalty in Vaelen. Though his parents forbade him from the Vanguard until he earned his **True-Bark**—a feat rarely achieved before the twentieth winter—Vaelen advanced through the ranks with unprecedented speed.

### II. The Silence of the Blood Falls

In the eleventh year of his life, driven by a blind confidence, Vaelen absconded from his family den under the cover of a moonless night. He ascended alone to the **Crimson Cleft**, intent on performing the **Rite of the Blood-Mist Span**—a feat of weaving considered impossible for an uninitiated cub.

Vaelen successfully entered the "Vocal Wall," a deep, synesthetic trance where the Weaver’s voice dictates the physical properties of nature. As he generated the _Song of the Initiate_, the sentient timber of the canyon responded, weaving a bridge over the deadly falls.

Alerted by the resonance of the song, his Root-Kin (parents) pursued him in a panic, fearing the boy would lose control and fall. Arriving at the cliff’s edge and seeing Vaelen precariously suspended on the half-formed span, they rushed onto the weaving boughs to retrieve him.

Deep within the delirium of the Vocal Wall, Vaelen perceived their arrival not as a rescue, but as a hallucination—a spiritual manifestation of parental pride conjured by the magic of the trial. Believing them to be visions, Vaelen ceased his song to reach out and embrace the apparitions.

The moment his vocalization stopped, the weave lost its integrity. As his hand met the solid fur of his mother’s shoulder, the horrific reality shattered his trance: they were not spirits, but flesh and blood. The bridge instantly dissolved into splinters. Vaelen, anchored by his own claws to the cliffside, could only watch as the timber gave way, dropping his parents into the abyss of the **Blood Falls**.

In a desperate bid to recover them, Vaelen did not sing; he roared a command of such raw, kinetic force that it knotted the roots of the canyon, creating an earthen dam that choked the waterfall entirely.

It was only the magical intervention of **Auranythe** that broke his catatonic state and prevented the backed-up waters from flooding the **Giant Den** of Arktos-Thur. When Vaelen awoke fourteen days later, the concussive force of that final, agonizing wail had shredded his vocal cords. The boy who had commanded the earth to stop had lost the ability to speak to it forever.

### III. The Apprenticeship and the Sentinel

Despite his mute affliction, Vaelen returned to the **Ur-Soma** (Mother Tree) six months later. Communicating through resonance rather than voice, he passed the **Trial of the First Mantle**, becoming the youngest Master Weaver in history. He was assigned as a "Root-Crosser"—an Ursine designated to work alongside other races—and apprenticed to the legendary **[[Ghalanduroth]]**.

The pair served with distinction, most notably during the **Relief of [[Liman]]**, where Vaelen’s "hum" was utilized to shear a tidal wave, saving the port city. However, their partnership ended in tragedy during the **Ambush at the Silent Copse**.

While escorting Auranythe near **Cor-Mesa**, the weavers were ambushed by a Northern Firemancer. The grove, a sacred burial ground of **Sentinel-Boles**, had been desecrated. During the skirmish, Ghalanduroth was mortally wounded by a _Cinder-Trail_ curse while protecting the young master.

With the High Marches overrun, the dying elder requested Vaelen’s aid in performing the **Final Rooting**. Ghalanduroth’s final recorded words, now known as the _Lament of Mirror Lake_, marked the transition:

> _"I have listened to the music of the bough for a hundred winters. Now, I shall become the instrument. Do not mourn the silence of my voice, for I go to join the choir that holds the forests of Sadhana together."_

Vaelen guided his master’s transformation into a Sentinel-Bole, while Auranythe wove a perennial stream to water the new tree. This event marked the end of Vaelen’s childhood and the beginning of his solitary mastery.

### IV. The Tower and the War of Glass

For five years, Vaelen and Auranythe constructed the great **Vanguard Tower**. Vaelen grew a core of **Iron-Heartwood** directly from the lakebed, while Auranythe encased it in a prism of reinforced glass. The structure was a symbiotic marvel, serving as a lens for magic and a fortress of living wood.

However, the **Ephemero Age** ended in cataclysm in the year **6340 EA**. The Iron Pass fell to the Northern Host, and the Vanguard Tower was toppled. The subsequent conflict, the **Battle of Shard Glass**, saw the destruction of the region's great cities.

Standing amidst the ruins of the tower and the body of Auranythe, Vaelen witnessed the aftermath. The intense heat of dragon-fire and the magical collapse had fused the sandy basin into a jagged floor of obsidian and crystal.

### V. The Turning of Ages (The Glass Census)

The sight of the crystallized lake fulfilled a prophecy Ghalanduroth had seen in a dream moments before his death. As the survivors of **Mar-Anur** gathered on the reflective surface, they saw not the mud of war, but the reflection of the sky—a symbol of a new beginning.

Vaelen, the last architect of the old world, took a silent vow to rebuild. This moment is historically designated as **The Glass Census**, marking the official end of the Ephemero Age and the dawn of the **Sadhana Age (1 S.A.)**. The lake was renamed **Mirror Lake**, and Vaelen became its silent guardian, ensuring the sacrifices of the Vanguard would be the foundation of the new era.
