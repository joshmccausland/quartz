The class reached its legendary peak with **High-Chieftain Veris-Thur**. His feat at the Iron Pass—weaving a massive living wall while simultaneously maintaining the "Singing" across four distinct armies—remains the standard by which all current Weavers are measured. Because of him, the season of **The Waking** is dedicated to this craft, as it is the time when the sap flows fastest and the wood is most "vocal".

### **Distinction of Tiers**

- **Root-Callers**: Apprentices who manage the foundational growth and the clearing of the **Marrow Path**.
    
- **Core-Weavers**: The primary architects of the city’s residential and trade districts.
    
- **Vanguard Weavers**: The elite class (like Veris-Thur) trained specifically for the defensive "Singing" of battle-walls and bridge-binding.
