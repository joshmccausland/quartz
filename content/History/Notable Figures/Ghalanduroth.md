#### **Linguistic Roots in the Elder Ursine**

- **Gha-lan**: A compound root meaning "The Long-Stride" or "He who reaches beyond the canopy".
    
- **du-roth**: An ancient suffix for "Solid Ground" or "Stone-Bound Root," signifying a traveler who never loses their foundation.

### **Lore Profile: Gha-lan-du-roth, The World-Stitcher**

Gha-lan-du-roth is the most senior **Root-Crosser** currently active in the **Sadhana Age**. While other Weavers focus on the internal "Song" of the forest, his life's work has been the **"Song of the Granite-Join"**—the art of weaving sentient vines into human stonework to make cities like **Cor-Mesa** earthquake-proof.

- **The Bark-Clad Mantle**: His armor is distinct; it is not the deep reddish-brown of the Blood Falls, but a pale, sun-bleached grey, reflecting his years spent in the open plains and coastal salt-air of the **Central Vale**.
    
- **The Diplomatic Feat**: He is credited with the "Aura-Binding," where he helped the humans of **Liman** grow a living sea-wall of iron-hard timber to protect their harbor from the winter storms of **Fel-Sadhana**.
    
- **The Scholar's Burden**: He carries a massive staff made from a scion of the **Ur-Soma**, etched with the runes of every human city he has visited.
