- **Ephemeros Age (E.A.):** To the Latecomers, this is pre-history—the thousands of years when the original inhabitants named the land and established kingdoms across the continent.

- **The Zero Point:** The day the first elven ships landed at [[Mar-Anur]]. This "Year 0" marks the moment history began to be recorded by the *Latecomers* and what is known as The Sadhana Age (S.A.).

# Seasons

The seasons on [[Sadhana]] are split into four distinct cycles. The four cycles of the year are named for the **Vanguard of the Iron Pass**, the legendary coalition that prevented the total desolation of the Central Vale during the **Ephemero Age** (E.A.).

- **High-Chieftain [[Veris-Thur]]** (Ursine Weaver)
- **[[Auranythe]]** (Human Mage) glass-tower stood upon the shores of **Mirror Lake**.
- **Lord [[Wynnharrow]]**, (Human Warrior) the Warden of **Clavis** who held the "Bridge of Binding" for forty nights against the Wyvern 
- **General [[Fel-Sadhana]]**, the unified commander who led the **Four Armies of the Vale** to the final, crushing victory at the High Marches.

#### Veris (Spring) - "The Waking"

As the first light of the new year touches the High Marches, the [[The Living Wood|Vael-Soma]] begins its annual "Ascent." During **The Waking**, a deep, low-frequency thrumming can be heard within the forests of **Arktos-Thur** as the sap begins to pulse upward through the sentient trunks. This is a time of "Weaving," where the Ursine singers guide the new growth of the city’s parapets, shaping living wood with ancient melodies before the timber hardens.
#### Aura (Summer) - "The Warming

**The Warming** is the season of peak vitality and the height of the **[[Mar-Anur]]** trade. The southern winds—the _Auras_—blow steadily through the [[Aethel-Vard]] bringing warmth that turns the Central Vale into a lush sea of gold. In the city of **Liman**, the "Festival of the First Anchor" is held to celebrate the landing of the Latecomers, while in the north, the heat causes the iron-veins of the mountains to expand, slightly increasing the flow of water into the [[Gula-Vael]].
#### Wynn (Autum) - "The Turning"

During **The Turning**, the atmospheric pressure shifts, causing the red mists of the **Blood Falls** to descend from the peaks and settle heavily into the valleys. The foliage of the Living Wood does not merely wither; it glows with a bioluminescent amber hue before the trees enter their "Great Silence". For the Ursine, this is the season of **The Sanguine Vigil**, a time of deep ancestral reflection as the world is bathed in the haunting, iron-scented glow of the autumn fogs.
#### Fel (Winter) - "The First-Frost"

As **The First-Frost** takes hold, the sap of the [[The Living Wood|Vael-Soma]] thickens and retreats into the deep roots, causing the sentient structures of the cities to become as hard and cold as iron. The **[[Marrow Path]]** often glimmers with a crystalline rime, and travel between the hubs of **Cor-Mesa** and the coast becomes treacherous. It is a time of "Fortress-Dwelling," where the hearths of Sadhana are the true centers of the world, and the people look toward the the stars and await the return of Veris.

# Days of the Week

There are 5 days of the week and based on different races, those days of the week could look differently. See below for a reference of how the days look for each race.

#### The Ursine Week
For the Ursine people of [[Thurn|Arktos-Thur]] time is a rhythmic pulse, measured by the vitality of the sentient timber and the flow of the crimson falls. Their names for the days are not mere labels but sacred stations of a cycle.

| **Day** | **Name**      | **Significance**                                                                                                                    |
| ------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**   | **Waking**    | Commemorating the "Exodus" and the first moment the Ursine pioneers touched the Living Wood.                                        |
| **2**   | **Exchange**  | The time of the "Trade-Flow" through Cor-Mesa, acknowledging the necessity of the world beyond the wood.                            |
| **3**   | **Coalesce**  | A centering point for the community; the gathering of the dens to weave new parapets or mend the sentient structures.               |
| **4**   | **Sanguine**  | The most sacred station, dedicated to the **Vael Katarak** (Blood Falls) and the iron-rich memory of the ancestors.                 |
| **5**   | **Ascension** | The final rest; a day spent on the high balconies of the fortress, observing the celestial movements and the silence of the forest. |

#### The Week of the Mar-Anur
The **Latecomers** or [[The Mar-Anur]] maintain a more pragmatic, navigation-based view of time, rooted in their arrival [[Central Vale]] at the [[Bay of Anura]]. First establishing [[Mar-Anur]] in Year 0 of the **Sadhana Age**. (S.A.)

| **Day** | **Name**     | **Significance**                                                                                                  |
| ------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1**   | **Mooring**  | Recalling the first anchor dropped at the southern tip; a time of new beginnings and labor.                       |
| **2**   | **Fair**     | Marking the peak of the markets where the bounty of the Sea-Sky people meets the crafts of the mainland.          |
| **3**   | **Kindred**  | A day for connection and the strengthening of bloodlines and trade-pacts.                                         |
| **4**   | **Venture**  | A time for exploration and the pushing of boundaries, whether into the Deep Vale or along the coast.              |
| **5**   | ** Horizon** | A day of quiet reflection on the setting sun, acknowledging the vast distances crossed and the new world claimed. |
