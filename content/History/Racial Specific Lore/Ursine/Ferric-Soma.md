 **The Iron-Vein Tempering**: This is the technical mastery of the "Red Flow." Using raw iron-concentrates from the [[Blood Falls|Vael Katarak]], initiates learn to "temper" the wood. By singing the iron particles into the pores of the timber, they create [[Ferric-Soma]]—wood that retains its cellular life but possesses the tensile strength and hardness of forged steel.
