- **Gula-Vael**

- **Common Name:** **The Gullet**
- **Etymology:**

- **Gula:** (Latin) Meaning **"Throat"** or **"Gullet."**
- **Vael:** The spirit or life-force.

- **Lore:** Gritty and visceral. It implies that everything the kingdom "consumes" must pass through this narrow waterway.
