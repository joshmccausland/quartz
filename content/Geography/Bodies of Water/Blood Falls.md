## Meaning: 
The term is used doubly. It is of fear for outsiders, but for the Ursine it is the lifeblood for the city of Aktos-Thur. 
## Entymology 
**Vael
Old Norse / Germanic** _Valr / Vala_Historically refers to **"The Slain"** or **"Chosen"** (as in _Valhalla_).

Katarak
Ancient Greek. Katarraktes Literally translates to **"Down-Smasher"** or **"Down-Rushing."** This is the root of the modern English word _Cataract_ (a massive waterfall).
