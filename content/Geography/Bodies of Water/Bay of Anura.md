## Meaning: 
**The Lore:** When the first Mariner ships entered this bay, the water was so still that the eastern peaks of the **High Marches** were perfectly reflected on the surface. They felt they were sailing not on water, but through the stars of the lower heavens.
## Entymology 
From the Latin _speculum_ and Greek _ouranos_.
Specul_ (Mirror) + _Anura_ (Sky/Heaven).
