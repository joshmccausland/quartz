## Meaning: 
The term is used doubly. It is of fear for outsiders, but for the Ursine it is the lifeblood for the city of Aktos-Thur. 
## Entymology 
- **Etymology:** _Vael_ (Blood/Essence) + _Rous_ (Greek: ρ˙ oυ~ς, meaning "stream" or "current").
