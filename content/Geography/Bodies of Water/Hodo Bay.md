- **Etymology:**

- **Nila:** (Sanskrit: नील) Meaning **"Dark Blue"** or **"Sapphire."**
- **Sagara:** (Sanskrit: सागर) Meaning **"Ocean"** or **"The Great Sea."**

- **Lore:** The Mariners believe the ocean isn't made of water, but of liquid starlight that fell from the sky (_Anura_) and pooled around the land.
