>  - It implies that this path is the most protected and vital route in the forest. It is the "inside" of the woods. Also considered dangerous to outsider

## Meaning: 
The term is used doubly. It is of fear for outsiders, but for the Ursine it is the lifeblood for the city of Aktos-Thur. 
## Entymology 
Vael_ (Essence) + _Ost_ (From the Latin/Greek _Osteo_, meaning "bone").
