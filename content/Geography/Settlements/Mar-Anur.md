- _Mar-Anur’_

- Entymology

- **Mar- (The Prefix)**

- This root is derived from the Latin **mare**, meaning **"sea."** * **Linguistic History:** It is one of the oldest Indo-European roots (_*mori_), appearing as _Muir_ in Celtic and _Mere_ in Old English.

- **Anura (The Suffix)**

- This is where the name gets its "High Elven" or "Celestial" flavor. It comes from the Greek root **Ouranos** (Οὐρανός). **Linguistic History:** **Ouranos** means **"Sky"** or **"Heaven."** It is the root for the planet _Uranus_ and the word _Uranic_(relating to the sky)
