## Description

Thurn is a sprawling fortress-city nestled within the heart of the [[The Living Wood]]. It serves as the ancestral seat of the Ursine people, marking the terminal point of the [[Marrow Path|Vael-Ost]] or commonly, [[Marrow Path|Marrow Path]]. To the Men and Elves who dare the [[Sadhana]] region, it is known simply as [[Thurn]]—a name whispered with the same reverence and dread one might give to a slumbering predator. ^b9a533

---
###  Geography & Environment

- [[The Living Wood]] **"Vael-Soma"** The city is not merely _in_ the forest; it is woven _from_ it. The structures are shaped from the iron-hard trunks of trees that still pulse with sap, their branches manipulated over centuries to form interlocking vaults and defensive parapets.
    
- **[[Blood Falls]]"Vael Katarak"** A massive, iron-rich waterfall that plunges from the cliffs above the city. The water carries a distinct crimson hue, creating a permanent red mist that hangs over the lower districts, giving the city its haunting, sanguine glow at sunset.
    
- **[[Marrow Path]]"Vael-Ost"** The primary artery leading into the city. It is a wide, cleared trail of white-stone and ancient roots that guided the first Ursine settlers through the otherwise impenetrable Sadhana wilds.
    

---
### History

Established in the [[Ephemeros]] Age, the city was founded when the first Ursine pioneers completed their exodus along the [[Marrow Path]]. Under the leadership of the chieftain [[Thur]], they carved a sanctuary out of the sentient timber. What began as a primitive clearing known as **Arktos-Thur** (The Gate of the Bear) has since evolved into a vertical titan of wood and stone, standing as a testament to the first victory over the wild heart of the Vael-Soma.

## Entymology

^3c034f

The name **Arktos-Thur** is a compound of the [[Ephemeros]] tongue, derived from ancient linguistic roots:

- **Arktos**: From the Greek _arktos_ (αρκτoς), meaning "bear."
- **Thur**: An archaic root signifying "stronghold," "tower," or "giant."

The literal translation is roughly **"The Giant Bear,"** though in older Ursine texts, it is often interpreted as **"The Giant Den"** referring to the city's role as a primal protector of the [[The Living Wood]].
