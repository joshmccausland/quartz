- **Aethel-Hearth**

- **Common Name: Aethel**
- **Etymology:**

- **Aethel:** (Old English root) Meaning **"Noble"** or **"Ancestral."**
- **Hearth:** (Germanic root) The floor of a fireplace; a symbol of one's home and family.

- **Lore:** Since you have **Clavis** (The Key) at the top and **Mesa** (The Center) in the middle, having a place simply called **Home** or **Hearth** at the bottom makes the geography feel very human. It’s the "Home-fire" of the kingdom.
