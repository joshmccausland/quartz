- **Halos-Ager: “The Salt Ledge” - Lavana** 

- **Etymology:**

- **Halos:** (Greek: _ἅλς_) Meaning **"Salt."**
- **Ager:** (Latin) Meaning **"Field"** or **"Region."**
- **Lavaṇa** (लवण) (Sanskrit) for Salt

- **Lore:** Named for the massive salt-pans that line the coast. The "Reach" refers to how the ocean air stretches inland, turning the grass white with salt spray.
