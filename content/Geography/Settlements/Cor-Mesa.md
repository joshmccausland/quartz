- **Cor-Meso “Cor-Mesa”**

- **Etymology:**

- **Cor:** (Latin: _Cor_) Meaning **"Heart."**  
- **Mesa:** (Latin/Spanish root) Meaning **"Table"** or **"Flat-topped Hill."**

- **Lore:** Sitting on a slightly elevated plateau in the middle of the Vale, **Mesa** is the "high ground" of the heartland. It is where the wealthiest merchant families build their estates to look out over their grain fields.
