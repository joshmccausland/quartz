- **Ananda-Giri** “**Ananda”**

- **Etymology:**

- **Ananda:** (Sanskrit: आनन्द) Meaning **"Bliss," "Joy,"** or **"Pure Happiness."**
- **Giri:** (Sanskrit: गिरि) Meaning **"Mountain."**

- **Lore:** It is said the air in **Ananda** is thinner and sweeter than anywhere else. The city is built vertically into the cliffs, catching the first and last light of every day.
