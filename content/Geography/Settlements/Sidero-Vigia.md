## Description

The dark, volcanic towers of **Sidero-Vigia** stand as a stark contrast to the living wood of [[Thurn]]. As the self-proclaimed "Protectors of the North," this city acts as a chokehold on the continent's most vital resources, governed by a regime that has spent centuries rewriting its own bloody origins. Known in the south as [[Sidero-Vigia|The Eye of the North]]

---
###  Geography & Environment

- **The Bridge of Binding:** A massive, ancient stone span crossing the **Gula-Vael**. Sidero-Vigia’s proximity to this bridge allows its leaders to act as the primary gatekeepers for all traffic between the Northern Uplands and the southern territories.

- **Volcanic Foundations:** The city is built directly into dark, volcanic stone, providing a naturally fortified position that is nearly impossible to breach by traditional siege.

- **Mineral Pipeline:** As the nearest major hub to the mining regions of [[Kastron|Kastron]] and [[Ferrum|Ferrum]] the city serves as the central refinery and distribution point for the North's iron and ore.

---
### History

The recorded history of Sidero-Vigia is a fabrication designed to hide a dark truth. Centuries ago, the city was inhabited by the early [[Ephemeros]], a peaceful culture with deep ties to the land. They were violently usurped by the ancestors of the current regime—vicious human leaders who claimed the volcanic towers for themselves. By slandering the name of the original inhabitants and sowing chaos, the current ruling class has successfully isolated the North, poisoning relations with the southern folk to maintain their grip on power.

**The Clavis Accord:** Relations with the southern city of [[Clavis]] are eternally tense, held together only by a fragile structural agreement.

**Exports:** Sidero-Vigia provides copper and mountain ore mined north of the **Gula-Vael**.

**Imports:** In exchange for their minerals, the city relies on Clavis for fresh crops, southern fish, and timber. This dependency is a source of constant political friction, as the North's leaders view this reliance as a strategic weakness.

## Entymology

The name **Sidero-Vigia** is a composite of the Ephemero tongue, retaining the linguistic markers of the early humans who first surveyed the volcanic heights:

- **Sidero**: From the Greek _sideros_ (σιˊδηρoς), meaning "iron." This refers both to the rich mineral deposits of the nearby mountains and the perceived "steely" resolve of the city's founders.

- **Vigia**: Derived from the Latin-root _vigil_ or the Greek-influenced _vigia_, meaning "watch," "sentry," or "lookout."

The literal translation is **"The Iron Watch."** In the modern era, the ruling faction has embellished this meaning to imply **"The Eye of the North,"** framing their tyranny as a necessary, unblinking vigil against the "chaos" of the southern lands.
