- **Chryso-Liman** “Golden Harbor”

- **Etymology:** Derived from the Greek _óryza_ (ὄρυζα), the ancient root for **"Rice"** or **"Grain."**
- **Lore** It sounds elegant and slightly exotic. It suggests a city with golden-roofed granaries and white stone docks.
