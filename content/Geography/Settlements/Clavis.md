## Description

While [[Sidero-Vigia]] represents the iron-fisted military might of the North, **Clavis** is the economic heart that keeps the continent beating. Nestled in [[The Iron Pass]], it serves as the ultimate bottleneck for survival.

---
###  Geography & Environment

 **The Iron Pass:** The city is strategically situated within this vital corridor, acting as the gateway between the resource-rich Northern Uplands and the fertile southern territories.

 **Breadbasket of the South:** Surrounded by the lands that provide the "life-blood" of the continent, Clavis controls the flow of fresh crops, southern fish, and essential timber.

**Proximity to the North:** Its location just south of the **Bridge of Binding** makes it the primary counterweight to the aggression of Sidero-Vigia.

---
### History

The relationship between Clavis and [[Sidero-Vigia]] is a study in "mutual loathing."

- **The Structural Agreement:** Clavis provides the food, wood, and timber that the volcanic North cannot produce for itself. In return, Clavis receives copper and mountain ore mined from the heights of the North.

- **A Fragile Peace:** While the leaders of Sidero-Vigia resent their dependency on Clavis for food, the "Blood-Lock" ensures that any military move against the south would result in the North's immediate starvation.

---
## Entymology

The name **Vael-Clavis** reflects the city's dual nature as both a life-giver and a gatekeeper:

- **Vael:** Derived from an **Old Norse** root meaning the "life-force" or "blood-line."
- **Clavis:** From the **Latin** meaning "Key" or "Lock."

In the common tongue, it is known as **The Blood-Lock**, a literal reminder that if this city closes its gates, the essential trade and food required for the continent's survival simply stop moving.
