
### **The Ferromancer (The Iron-Singer)**

While "Ferromancer" is the academic title, the common folk of the High Marches often refer to them as **"Iron-Singers"**because of the high-pitched, metallic hum produced when they vibrate the molecules of raw ore.

### **## 📜 Etymology & Lore**

- **Root:** From the Latin _ferrum_, meaning **"Iron."**
    
- **Scholarly Term:** **Sideromancy.** Derived from the Greek _sideros_ (σιˊδηρoς), linking them directly to the "Iron Watch" of **Sidero-Vigia**.
    
- **The Origin:** Most of these mages are born or trained in the volcanic heights of **Ferum** or **Kastron**, where the proximity to the earth's molten core makes the iron "softer" to their mental touch.

### **## 🛠 Class Abilities (for your Lore Note)**

You can add these specific details to a new note in your `03_Lore` folder titled **"Mage Class - Ferromancer"**:

- **Structural Warping:** They can "bend" the iron within the **Bridge of Binding**, allowing them to create temporary blockades or collapse sections of the bridge during an invasion.
    
- **The Sidero-Vigia Sentry:** Since the towers of Sidero-Vigia are reinforced with iron, Ferromancers can sense vibrations through the walls, making it impossible for anyone to sneak through the city.
    
- **Weapon Forging:** Unlike traditional blacksmiths, a Ferromancer can pull impurities out of iron with their mind, creating blades that never dull and armor that is as light as silk but harder than dragon-scale.
