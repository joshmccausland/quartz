The Weavers are the **Ears of the Forest**, a sacred order of [[Ursine]] who can hear the ancient thrum of the world. They do not command the forest, nor do they claim mastery over the timber; rather, they are the humble listeners who find the music already written within the grain of the **[[The Living Wood|Vael-Soma]]** and help it reach its intended shape.

It is recorded in the Archives of [[Thurn|Arktos-Thur]] in what is known as "The Weaver's Humility" or  _Doctrine of the Passive Ear_:

> "The tunes we hold are not our own. It is not our destiny to write the music, but to listen and feel the music that is written for us. Perfect harmony is only achieved by those can truly listen." 

### **The Art of Resonance**

A Weaver works through the **Art of Resonance**, matching the low-frequency vibrations of their own spirit to the deep, pulse of the sentient wood. 

### **The Legacy of the Shield**

While their craft is one of peace and harmony, history has shown that the Song can become the island's most formidable defense. 

During times of war, such as the **Great Wars of the Iron Pass**, Weavers, with the most famous **[[Veris-Thur]]** acted as front-line combat engineers, rapidly growing defensive spikes and barricades directly from the earth to crush invaders.

## **The Path of the Weaver**

A Weaver’s life is defined by the **Ages of Growth**, a twenty-year journey of resonance with the Living Wood. The body of the forest dictates the speed at which you move through these growth phases. The age brackets are estimates based on historical data of [[Ursine]] growth. As soon as weavers show aptitude enough to pass each tier, they move on immediately to the next regardless of age. The master weavers, consider the growth set by The Living Wood and the Ur-Soma. 
#### **Tier I: Sapling (Ages 0–10)**

In the early years, the Ursine youth are paired with "*[[Soma]]*"—young, flexible saplings that respond easily to high-pitched, simple vocalizations. These "Soma" are considered part of the "body" of the forest.

After they are able to bend these Soma they move on to their next test, domestic weaving.

Domestic Weaving focus on internal structures, coaxing thin branches into sturdy furniture, shelving, and intricate curtain-frames that grow directly from the walls of their dens. After they achieve this and pass their final test for structural integrity, they move on to "Trial of the Arrow" which is a critical milestone is weaving a single, seamless arrow from a living branch without breaking the grain, imbuing the wood with a natural tension that no manufactured bow can match. These arrows are highly sought after with humans and elves alike. After completing the "Trial of the Arrow" weavers move on to Root Calling.

#### **Tier II: Root Calling (Ages 10–16)**

As the Weaver’s voice deepens, they move from manipulating objects to manipulating environments.

- **Aero-Acoustics**: Students learn to "tunnel" the wind by weaving tree canopies into specific aerodynamic funnels. This provides natural ventilation for the lower districts of **Arktos-Thur** and can even be used to create "Wind-Whistles" that alert the city to approaching attacks from wyverns or other invaders.

- **Hydrological Rooting**: They study the deep-earth resonance, learning to shift the massive roots of the elder trees to redirect mountain streams or guide the iron-rich waters of the [[Blood Falls|Vael Katarak]] into the city's irrigation veins.

#### **Tier III: The Vanguard Initiate (Ages 16–20)**

The Initiate is no longer a student of the forest's whims; they are trained to be its iron-fisted defenders. They move to the **High-Resonance Districts**, where the trees are so old they have begun to "petrify" naturally, requiring immense vocal power and iron catalysts to move.

#### Bulwark-Weaving

Before the final initiate trial, initiates must master the arts of static defense.

- **Siege-Roots**: The **Siege-Roots** represent a profound mastery of the **Vanguard Initiate**, marking the transition from surface weaving to commanding the primordial strength of the **[[The Living Wood|Vael-Soma]]**. By driving roots deep into the ground, the initiate bypasses soft topsoil to strike the mountain’s granite bedrock, anchoring structures into the heart of **[[Sadhana]]**. This provides a Weaver with immediate personal sanctuary—erupting a protective barrier of iron-hard timber around themselves in the heat of battle. Siege Roots also serve as the primary reinforcement for a city's battle-walls. Once anchored, these defensive lines become immovable extensions of the forest itself, capable of withstanding the weight of a marching army or the concussive tremors of a wyvern assault without yielding.

- **The Iron-Vein Tempering**: This is the technical mastery of the "Red Flow." Using raw iron-concentrates from the [[Blood Falls|Vael Katarak]], initiates learn to "temper" the wood. By singing the iron particles into the pores of the timber, they create [[Ferric-Soma]]—wood that retains its cellular life but possesses the tensile strength and hardness of forged steel.

### **The Capstone Trial: The Rite of the Blood-Mist Span**

The culmination of the Vanguard Initiate’s training is not a classroom test, but a feat of engineering performed at the very brink of the [[Blood Falls|Vael Katarak]].

#### **The Setting: The Throat of the Falls**

The initiate is taken to the **Crimson Cleft**, the highest and most turbulent point of the falls. Here, the roar of the water is a "Vocal Wall," a deafening frequency that drowns out all but the most powerful songs. The iron-rich mist creates "Static Interference," making it almost impossible to maintain a steady resonance with the wood.

#### **The Trial**

The initiate must stand on a precipice and "call" to two ancient, iron-tempered boughs on opposite sides of the churning abyss.

1. **The Call**: They must project their voice through the roar of the falls to find the "Heart-Thrum" of the timber.

2. **The Binding**: They must weave these two massive boughs together over the roaring water to create a **Resonant Span**—a bridge capable of supporting a full Ursine war-party.

3. **The Tempering**: As the bridge forms, they must draw the iron-mist out of the air and "seal" the span while suspended over the drop.

**The Stakes**: Failure is rarely fatal, as Master Weavers stand by with "Safety-Tendrils," but a failed bridge means the initiate has "lost their voice". They are barred from the High-Resonance districts and relegated to the **Sanguine-Watch** or domestic maintenance, never permitted to seek the **Ur-Soma**.

---
### **The Final Test: The Trial of the First Mantle

Upon completion of The Blood-Mist trial, the initiate travels to the heart of [[The Living Wood]], just southeast of [[Thurn|Arktos-Thur]] to find the [[Ur-Soma]]—the "Mother Tree" from which all [[The Living Wood|Vael-Soma]] descended. [[Ur-Soma]] stands upon a small hill, overlooking the [[The Living Wood]]]], "protecting it" through the seasons.

The initiate must "sing" a section of the [[Ur-Soma]]’s bark—bark that has hardened over thousands of years into a substance as tough as dragon-scale—completely off the trunk. They must then replace it so perfectly that the tree "accepts" the graft back instantly, leaving no visible seam or scar.

- **Success**: If the tree accepts the work, a single strand of the bark is given to the weaver Weaver. The gift of "True-Bark".

- **Failure**: To fail is to be "Silenced," where the Weaver may no longer practice high-tier weaving and remains a permanent Root-Caller for the domestic districts.

#### **The Reward: The Bark-Clad Mantle**

The single strand of True-Bark is taken to the [[The Bark-Wrights|Ouro-Tanners]]. These masters of skin and sap assemble a set of **Bark-Anvil Armor**.

- **Properties**: Imbued with the "Life-Memory" of the Ur-Soma, this armor is weightless to the wearer but harder than plate. It pulses with a faint amber light during the season of **The Waking** and can actually "knit" itself back together if damaged in battle.

Upon donning the armor, the Ursine is named a **Master Weaver**.

### Master-Weaver Orders

Once an Ursine has proven they can "sing" the bark of the Mother Tree, they are no longer just a citizen; they are a vital organ of the city's living body. Their life is no longer their own; it is dedicated to serving The Living Wood, standing as the living shield between the civilized world and Sadhana. 

They choose an Order to focus their resonance for the remainder of their long life, or Song as they refer to their life. 

#### **I. The Shield-Boughs (Vanguard Masters)**

The elite military class, following the tradition of **Veris-Thur**. They are the only order permitted to carry "Vials of the Falls"—concentrated iron-water used to instantly petrify wood into an impenetrable shield.

- **Focus**: They specialize in "Rapid-Weaving" during the heat of battle.

- **Feats**: They can erupt jagged barricades of iron-hard timber from the earth in seconds or "Bridge-Bind" a chasm during battle.

#### **II. The Arbor-Wrights (Architectural Masters)**

The masters of the "Interior Pulse," responsible for the growth and expansion of **Thurn**.

- **Focus**: They design the sprawling vertical dwellings and Great Cafeterias that house the Ursine dens.

- **Feats**: Unlike apprentices, Arbor-Wrights can weave "Multi-Floor Boles," creating massive hollowed trunks that serve as residential towers with internal spiral staircases grown directly into the wood grain.

#### **III. The Sanguine-Watch (Maintenance Masters)**

The "Physicians of the Forest," whose work is often silent and invisible but essential to the city’s survival.

- **Focus**: They maintain the "Vael-Tension"—the balance between a structure’s stone-like durability and its biological flexibility.

- **Feats**: They monitor the nutrient flow of the **Vael Katarak** (Blood Falls) through the city's root-veins. During the season of **Fel-Sadhana** (Winter), they "sing" the city into its protective hibernation, ensuring the wood does not crack under the deep frost.

#### **IV. The Marrow-Binders (Medicinal Support)**

A sacred class that applies the principles of weaving to the Ursine body itself.

- **Focus**: Providing rapid support for the injured through "Living-Splints".

- **Feats**: They can weave a "Marrow-Casing" around a shattered limb—a lightweight, breathable bark-cast that pulses with sap-nutrients to accelerate bone healing. They also grow specialized "Support-Staffs" and crutches that respond to the wearer’s weight, making them far more effective than dead timber.

#### **V. The Root-Crossers (Diplomatic Masters)**

A rare and respected class that serves as the bridge between **Arktos-Thur** and the human/elven cities of the **Central Vale**.

- **Focus**: The "Great Exchange" program, sharing Ursine biological insight with allied nations.

- **Feats**: A Root-Crosser might spend a decade in **Cor-Mesa** or **Liman**, helping human architects reinforce their stone walls with "Binding-Vines" or teaching elven mages how to communicate with the local flora. In return, they bring back knowledge of metallurgy and star-charting to the Bear's Gate.

### **The Final Rooting**

> _"I have listened to the music of the bough for a hundred winters. Now, I shall become the instrument. Do not mourn the silence of my voice, for I go to join the choir that holds the mountain together."_ — **Ghalanduroth**, prior to his Rooting.

When a Weaver feels their internal rhythm slowing—a sensation known as the **Call of the Deep-Bole**—they begin their final journey. 

- **The Planting**: The Weaver chooses a location where the city or the forest requires eternal protection—often a gap in a defensive rampart, a thinning section of the canopy, or a sacred grove. They plant their feet into the earth and begin a low, continuous chant that vibrates through their entire skeletal structure.

- **The Descent of the Soul**: As the chant deepens, their feet begin to drive downward, bypassing the topsoil to strike granite bedrock, much like the **Siege-Roots** of an initiate. Their legs fuse together into a singular, sturdy **Bole** (trunk), and their arms reach toward the sky as **Primary Boughs**.

- **The Transmutation of the Mantle**: The **Bark-Anvil Armor** they have worn since their twentieth year does not fall away; instead, it expands and fuses with their flesh. The "True-Bark" of the **Ur-Soma** recognizes its source and begins to "knit" the Weaver's body into a magnificent, iron-hard tree.

### **The Sentinel Trees**

The trees that grow from the passing of a Weaver are distinct from the standard **Vael-Soma**. They are known as **Sentinel-Boles**, and they carry the physical and spiritual "echo" of the individual who formed them.

- **Iron-Heartwood**: Because Master Weavers have spent decades performing **Iron-Vein Tempering**, the trees they become are naturally infused with the metallic sediment of the **Blood Falls**. Their wood is rust-hued and nearly impossible to cut, serving as a permanent, living fortification.

- **The Whispering Groves**: It is whispered among the **Arbor-Wrights** that if one presses an ear to the trunk of a Sentinel Tree during the season of **The Waking**, you can still hear the faint, low-frequency hum of the Weaver’s original Song.

- **Ancestral Walls**: The very walls of **Arktos-Thur** are, in many places, composed of generations of Master Weavers who chose to "Root" themselves in a line. To attack the city is to attack the ancestors themselves, who stand in an eternal, interlocking embrace of iron-hard timber.
