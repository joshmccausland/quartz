They are the only ones permitted to touch the "True-Bark" gifted by the Mother Tree. They treat the bark with a mixture of leviathan-oil (from the Mar-Anur trade) and crushed iron-ore, making the **Bark-Anvil Armor** both flexible and indestructible.

 **Properties**: Imbued with the "Life-Memory" of the Ur-Soma, this armor is weightless to the wearer but harder than plate. It pulses with a faint amber light during the season of **The Waking** and can actually "knit" itself back together if damaged in battle.

Importance in the Trail of the First Mantle

Whenever they pass their initiate test, the initiate travels to the heart of [[The Living Wood]], just southeast of [[Thurn|Arktos-Thur]] to find the [[Ur-Soma]]—the "Mother Tree" from which all Vael-Soma descended. Ur-Soma stands upon a small hill, overlooking the Living Wood, "protecting it" through the seasons.

The initiate must "sing" a section of the [[Ur-Soma]]’s bark—bark that has hardened over thousands of years into a substance as tough as dragon-scale—completely off the trunk. They must then replace it so perfectly that the tree "accepts" the graft back instantly, leaving no visible seam or scar.

- **Success**: If the tree accepts the work, a single strand of the bark is given to the weaver Weaver. The gift of "True-Bark".

- **Failure**: To fail is to be "Silenced," where the Weaver may no longer practice high-tier weaving and remains a permanent Root-Caller for the domestic districts.
