### 1. King Dolonor (The Rust-Liar)

- **Etymology:** A combination of the Latin-root _dolosus_ (deceitful/crafty) and the Greek-root _nosos_ (disease) or simply a corruption of "Dolor" (pain/sorrow).
    
- **The Vibe:** It sounds like a name whispered in the dark. It implies a ruler who has allowed his soul to "rust" through centuries of lies.
    
- **The Gross Factor:** Describe him as having "oxidized blood" or skin the color of wet iron.
### **The Sovereign of Sidero-Vigia**

**Full Name:** Malaris Dolonor **Regnal Title:** The Sidero-Archon (or "The Rust-Sovereign")

---

### **## 📜 The Scholarly Breakdown**

Following the linguistic patterns of **Nila-Sagara**, the name is constructed from roots of illness and trickery:

- **Malaris:** Derived from the Latin-root _malaria_ (bad air) or _mal-_ (evil/illness). It suggests a leader who is not a healer of his people, but a fever that has gripped the North for centuries.
    
- **Dolonor:** A composite of the Greek **Dolos** (δoˊλoς), meaning "guile," "deceit," or "treachery," and the root **Nos**(disease/rust).
    

**Literal Meaning:** _"The Deceitful Sickness"_ or _"He who Rules through Rust and Lies."_

---

### **## 👁️ Physical Description (The "Gross" Factor)**

Malaris does not look like the strong human kings of the South. His long reign—sustained by the very minerals his people mine—has left him physically "oxidized":

- **The Rust-Tongue:** His title comes from a literal affliction; his saliva is iron-rich and acidic, staining his teeth a dark, metallic orange and causing his words to sound wet and grinding.
    
- **Pitted Skin:** His flesh is described as having the texture of "cankered iron," full of small, darkened pits that weep a thin, gray fluid.
    
- **The Usurper’s Garb:** He wears heavy plates of black obsidian over his decaying form, less for protection and more to hold his brittle body together.
    

---

### **## 📜 Lore: The Deceit of the North**

Malaris is the architect of the **"Great Slander."** After his ancestors violently usurped the **Ephemero humans**, he spent generations rewriting the history of the **High Marches**:

- He tells his people that the South (Clavis and Mar-Anura) intends to starve the North by withholding crops.
    
- He claims the **Ephemero** were not people, but "shadow-pests" that had to be cleared for the "true men" of the North to survive.
    
- His control over the iron mines of **Kastron** and **Ferum** allows him to keep his populace armed and angry, ensuring they never look too closely at the rot sitting on the throne in **Sidero-Vigia**.
